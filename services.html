Services

Everything under the ridge cap.

From a single missing shingle to a full tear-off, one crew handles the whole roof system — not just the parts that are easy to sell.

01

Full Roof Replacement

Complete tear-off and reroof with architectural or three-tab shingle, synthetic underlayment, and ice-and-water shield at every eave and valley.

02

Storm Damage Repair

Hail and wind assessment, emergency tarping within 24 hours, and insurance-ready photo documentation from the first visit.

03

Standing Seam Metal

Concealed-fastener metal roofing for steep pitches, additions, and porches — built for a 50-year service life with minimal maintenance.

04

Roof Inspections

A 32-point check covering flashing, ridge caps, valleys, pipe boots, and attic ventilation, written up in plain language with photos.

05

Gutters & Flashing

Seamless aluminum gutter runs, drip edge, and step or counter flashing sized and sealed to the roof they're protecting.

06

New Construction

Roof systems specified and installed alongside builders, from framing inspection through final ridge cap and warranty registration.

07

Roof Maintenance Plans

Annual inspection, gutter clean-out, and minor repair bundled together — catch small problems before they become tear-off jobs.

08

Skylights & Ventilation

Skylight install and replacement, ridge and soffit venting sized correctly to stop attic condensation and ice damming.

09

Chimney & Flashing Repair

Rebuilt crickets, resealed counter-flashing, and mortar repair where the chimney meets the roof deck — a common, under-quoted leak point.
